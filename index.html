Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about working with Forsyth? Here are answers to the things our clients ask most before starting a project.

1. How long does a typical web design project take?

Most projects are completed within 4–8 weeks depending on scope and complexity. A 5-page Starter site typically takes 3–4 weeks, while a full Studio build with custom features can run 8–12 weeks. We'll give you a detailed timeline at the start of your project.

2. How many rounds of revisions are included?

All plans include two rounds of revisions at both the design and development stages. We find this is more than enough to get things exactly right. Additional revisions can be added at an hourly rate if needed.

3. What platforms and technologies do you build on?

We primarily build with HTML/CSS/JS, React, and Next.js for custom sites. For clients who need content management, we integrate with WordPress, Webflow, or headless CMS platforms like Sanity or Contentful — whatever best suits your team.

4. Will my website be mobile-friendly?

Absolutely — mobile responsiveness is non-negotiable in every project we deliver. We design and test across all major screen sizes and devices to ensure a flawless experience for every visitor, regardless of how they browse.

5. Do you offer ongoing support after launch?

Yes. All plans include a post-launch support period (3 months for Growth, 6 months for Studio). We also offer monthly maintenance retainers for clients who want ongoing updates, performance monitoring, and priority access to our team.
